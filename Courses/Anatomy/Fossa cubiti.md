# Fossa cubiti

> Starter note: verify details against your current course materials and textbook.

## Overview
The cubital fossa is a triangular depression on the anterior aspect of the elbow.

## Boundaries
- **Superolateral:** brachioradialis
- **Superomedial:** pronator teres
- **Superior:** an imaginary line between the epicondyles
- **Floor:** brachialis and supinator
- **Roof:** skin, superficial fascia and deep fascia, reinforced by the bicipital aponeurosis

## Important contents
A useful lateral-to-medial memory aid is **TAN**:
- **T**endon of biceps brachii
- **A**rtery: brachial artery and its termination
- **N**erve: median nerve

The radial nerve lies more laterally in the region. The median cubital vein is superficial, in the roof, not a deep content.
