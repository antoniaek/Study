# Dental Studies Hub

Use this hub to connect foundational science to dental practice.

## Clinical connection template
For each important concept, ask:
- What is the basic science?
- What is the structure or process?
- Why does it matter in dentistry?
- What is a common misconception?
- Which other notes explain the concept?
