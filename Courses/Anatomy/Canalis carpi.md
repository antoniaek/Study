# Canalis carpi

> Starter note: check the exact boundaries and contents against your course materials.

## Core idea
The carpal tunnel is an osteofibrous passage at the wrist.

## What to learn
- Bony boundaries and the flexor retinaculum
- Flexor tendons passing through the tunnel
- Median nerve
- Structures that pass superficial to the flexor retinaculum rather than through the tunnel
- Clinical relevance, including median nerve compression
