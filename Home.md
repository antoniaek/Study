## Learning loop
1. **Capture:** write down the idea in your own words.
2. **Clarify:** identify the core concept and what remains unclear.
3. **Connect:** link it to related concepts with `[[wikilinks]]`.
4. **Retrieve:** test yourself without looking at the note.
5. **Review:** update the note when you discover something new.
